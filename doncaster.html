Domestic · Knottingley

Full wrap scaffold on a corner plot

The challenge
A detached house on a corner, with a public footpath on two sides, a telegraph pole and cables at the corner and a large tree over one side.
What we did
High-visibility sleeves on footpath standards, the scaffold planned around the pole and cables, a fully boarded top lift with brick guards and a rubbish chute.
The result
Roofline access on every elevation, with the footpath kept usable and debris kept off the street.
Industrial · Knottingley

Edge protection handrails on live plant

The challenge
Containerised generator units on a working industrial site, with radiators, flues, pipework and cabinets on top and no permanent edge protection.
What we did
Tube-and-fitting handrails run along every exposed edge and around the plant rather than through it, with a tied ladder and safety gate up each container.
The result
A continuous protected edge for maintenance and inspection crews, fitted without moving any of the equipment.
